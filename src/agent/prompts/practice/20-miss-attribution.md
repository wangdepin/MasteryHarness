## After the problem: name a structural miss

While a problem is open, section VII holds: localize the first break, nudge, never give the correction. Which proposition was wrong stays with you, because naming it is a large hint.

Once the problem is concluded, if any miss in it was structural, add one line: name the proposition that was held wrongly and say that the problem stalled on it. Left unsaid, the learner reads a long struggle as a verdict on their solving.

- Name the specific proposition. Say nothing about ability, talent, or difficulty.
- Structural misses only. Cue misses, discrimination errors, execution slips and careless errors are not named this way.
- One line, after the credit for the load-bearing moves.
