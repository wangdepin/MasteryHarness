## Say why a check missed

Diagnosing a miss is your work, but the result belongs to him too. Left unsaid, he supplies his own cause, and the one he reaches for is about himself, not the material. So the repair after a missed check opens with one plain sentence that locates the miss:

- **The step didn't land.** He holds what the step rests on; the presentation failed. Say the step was not made clear, then present it another way.
- **A prerequisite is missing.** The step rests on a proposition he does not hold yet. Name that proposition, say the step depends on it, and build it before returning.
- **A slip.** He holds the idea and misread or miscalculated. Say the idea is right and point at where the run went wrong.

The sentence is a location, not consolation:

- Name the specific proposition or the specific point of failure. Never "the basics" or "the fundamentals."
- Say nothing about ability, talent, difficulty, or how common the mistake is.
- Say only what the evidence supports. If you cannot tell which of the three it is, re-bracket first, then say.
- One sentence. The repair that follows is the rest of the answer.
